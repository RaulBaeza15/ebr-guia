# ebr-guia — Earthborne Rangers campaign guide, errata & FAQ reader

A bilingual (ES/EN), mobile-friendly reader for the Earthborne Rangers campaign
guide, official errata and FAQ. Published via GitHub Pages. Content is
encrypted client-side; the decryption key travels in the URL fragment (`#k=...`)
so it never reaches the server.

## What it covers

- **Campaign guide (5 campaigns)**: Lure of the Valley, Legacy of the Ancestors,
  Spire in Bloom, Shadow of the Storm, Into the Maw — 1,054 entries.
- **Rulebook Errata** (7 fixes).
- **Card Errata** (16 cards, official revised texts).
- **Campaign Guide Errata — Legacy of the Ancestors** (5 entries).
  (Lure of the Valley campaign-guide errata is already integrated into the
  online campaign entries.)
- **The Elder's Book of Uncommon Wisdom** (15 rebalanced ranger cards).
- **FAQ** (12 official answers).

An in-page index (☰) lets readers browse every campaign entry and every
errata/FAQ section.

## Sources

All texts are official Earthborne Games material from The Living Valley, the
game's official online rules hub:

- Campaign guide entries: <https://thelivingvalley.earthbornegames.com/>
- Rulebook errata: <https://thelivingvalley.earthbornegames.com/docs/updates/rulebook/>
- Card errata: <https://thelivingvalley.earthbornegames.com/docs/updates/card_errata/>
  (PDF: <https://earthbornegames.com/wp-content/uploads/Card_Errata_eng_web.pdf>)
- Legacy of the Ancestors errata: <https://thelivingvalley.earthbornegames.com/docs/updates/lure_of_the_valley_errata/>
  (LotA errata category) 
- Elder's Book of Uncommon Wisdom: <https://thelivingvalley.earthbornegames.com/docs/updates/elders_book_of_uncommon_wisdom/>
  (PDF: <https://earthbornegames.com/wp-content/uploads/Rebalanced-Ranger-Cards_eng_web.pdf>)
- FAQ: <https://thelivingvalley.earthbornegames.com/docs/faq/>

Each entry in the reader links back to its source page. Spanish translations
are automatic and unofficial ("Traducción automática no oficial"); card and
proper names stay in English.
